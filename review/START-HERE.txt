Sri Basavalinga Avadhoota Ashram — website design review
=========================================================

Open index.html in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
No internet connection or server is needed — every page, font and photo
is embedded in the files.

Pages included:
  index.html      Home
  about.html      About Guru
  ashram.html     The Ashram
  teachings.html  Teachings
  gallery.html    Gallery (category filters + full-screen photo viewer)
  events.html     Events
  contact.html    Contact
  donate.html     Donate
  privacy.html    Privacy

Working in this review copy: page-to-page navigation, mobile menu, photo
viewer (arrow keys / on-screen arrows / Esc), gallery category filters,
donation amount selection.

Not active here: form submission and the payment gateway. Those run on the
live site; in this copy the forms simply show a note. This is a design
review build, not the deployed website.

Content shown (events, photographs, quotes, phone numbers) is from the
current site content and can be updated before launch.
